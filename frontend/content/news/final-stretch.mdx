**Lots of sleepless nights, testing, brainstorming, and months of hard work brought us here.**

Now it’s countdown time: polish the rough edges, fix the bugs, update the docs—and try not to discover a whole new category of bugs while doing it. Here’s what we’ve built so far.

## Platform

> **[Vault](https://vital-sandbox.com/vault)** — Publish and maintain community resources right on the site. No manual forks or Git archaeology.
>
> **[Workspace](https://vital-sandbox.com/workspace)** — Manage servers, resources, applications, and staff reviews in one place.
>
> **[Benchmarks](https://vital-sandbox.com/benchmarks)** — Live Lua vs. GDScript results from deployed builds. No benchmark theater; try them yourself.
>
> **[Studio](https://vital-sandbox.com/studio)** — Create logos, banners, and social assets. Your promo art deserves better than a stretched PNG from 2014.
>
> **[Docs](https://vital-sandbox.com/docs)** — API references, enums, signals, guides, and server configuration.
> 
> **[Roadmap](https://vital-sandbox.com/roadmap)** — See the status of every system.
>
> **Masterlist** — Server heartbeats, logos, banners, and up to five genre tags. Tokens expire after 14 days of inactivity. Submissions are reviewed by humans; we’re keeping the AI slop out.

## Runtime

> - Console with structured logging and client/server commands.
> - Isolated Lua environments for each resource, with dependency resolution and start, stop, and restart controls.
> - Hash-verified asset streaming and concurrent client downloads.
> - Neon-logo splash screen with CRT scanlines, plus an in-game main menu.
> - Windows builds are shipping; Linux is still on the list.

## Core

> - Engine, display, screenshots, MySQL database support, and asynchronous queries.
> - Discord Rich Presence.
> - Models with GLB support, animation layers, blendshapes, and network synchronization.
> - Camera, webview, canvas, SVG, images, fonts, textures, and render targets.
> - GLSL shaders from files or source, including CanvasItem, Spatial, and post-processing shaders.

## Graphics

> - Physical, panoramic, and procedural skies; SDFGI, SSAO, SSR, SSIL, glow, and volumetric fog.
> - FXAA, TAA, and MSAA, plus color grading.
> - Directional, point, spot, and area lights.
> - GPU particles with shapes, trails, materials, mesh particles, and signals.

## Physics

> - Rigid, static, character, animatable, and vehicle bodies—with wheels.
> - Box, sphere, capsule, cylinder, world-boundary, separation-ray, and mesh collision shapes.
> - Raycasts, point and shape intersections, motion casts, and trigger areas.
> - Runtime collision generation and a physics debugger.
> - Up to **65,535 isolated virtual dimensions**, with full replication. GTA-style world separation, without one giant shared-space traffic jam.

## Misc

> - **Audio:** 2D and 3D sources, effect buses, and multi-format streaming.
> - **Utilities:** Math, strings, tables, timers, promises, threads, easing, files, HTTP, crypto, compression, events, input, keybinds, and performance monitors.
> - **Networking:** ENet transport with reliable RPC, ordered sync, auto-reconnect, and RTT.
> - **Replication:** Entity lifecycle, authority, late-join state dumps, and client interpolation. Physics, vehicle wheels, dimensions, animation layers, and reparenting sync too.
