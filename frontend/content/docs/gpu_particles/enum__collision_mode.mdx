---
title: particle.gpu.collision_mode
description: Enumeration of what a particle does when it touches the world
badge: client
---

### Values

| Constant | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `particle.gpu.collision_mode.DISABLED` | Particles ignore the world collider |
| `particle.gpu.collision_mode.RIGID` | Particles land on the surface and bounce or slide, depending on the bounce and friction |
| `particle.gpu.collision_mode.HIDE` | Particles disappear the moment they touch the surface — rain and anything that should splash and vanish |
