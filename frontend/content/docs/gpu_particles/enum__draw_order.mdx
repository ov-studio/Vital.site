---
title: particle.gpu.draw_order
description: Enumeration of the order in which particles are drawn
badge: client
---

### Values

| Constant | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `particle.gpu.draw_order.INDEX` | Particles are drawn in the order they were emitted |
| `particle.gpu.draw_order.LIFETIME` | Particles are drawn from the youngest to the oldest |
| `particle.gpu.draw_order.REVERSE_LIFETIME` | Particles are drawn from the oldest to the youngest |
| `particle.gpu.draw_order.VIEW_DEPTH` | Particles are drawn from the farthest to the closest to the camera |

---

### Examples

```lua lineNumbers title="Draw the closest particles last"
local entity = particle.gpu.create()

entity:set_draw_order(particle.gpu.draw_order.VIEW_DEPTH)
```
