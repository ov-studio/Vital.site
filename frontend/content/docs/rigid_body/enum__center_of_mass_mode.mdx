---
title: physics.rigid.center_of_mass_mode
description: Enumeration of modes controlling how a rigid body's center of mass is determined
badge: client
---

### Values

| Constant | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `physics.rigid.center_of_mass_mode.AUTO` | Center of mass is calculated automatically from the attached collision shapes |
| `physics.rigid.center_of_mass_mode.CUSTOM` | Center of mass is set manually |

---

### Examples

```lua lineNumbers title="Reference the center of mass mode enum"
core.engine.print("info", physics.rigid.center_of_mass_mode.AUTO)
```
