---
title: core.camera.doppler_tracking
description: Enumeration of Doppler tracking modes available for the camera
badge: client
---

### Values

| Constant | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `core.camera.doppler_tracking.DISABLED` | Disables Doppler effect tracking |
| `core.camera.doppler_tracking.IDLE_STEP` | Tracks Doppler effect during idle process steps |
| `core.camera.doppler_tracking.PHYSICS_STEP` | Tracks Doppler effect during physics process steps |

---

### Examples

```lua lineNumbers title="Enable physics-step Doppler tracking on a camera"
local entity = core.camera.create()

entity:set_doppler_tracking(core.camera.doppler_tracking.PHYSICS_STEP)
```
