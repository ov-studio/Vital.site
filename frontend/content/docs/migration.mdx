## Overview

These guides are for developers running an active multiplayer server on an existing platform — primarily MTA, SA-MP, FiveM, or Roblox — who want to move their gamemode to **Vital.sandbox**.

---

## Who should migrate

| Signal | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| Hit scripting limits | Your platform's language or threading model is blocking features you want to build |
| Closed or abandoned platform | SA-MP is unmaintained; FiveM carries Take-Two legal risk; MTA's network layer is closed |
| Plugin dependency hell | Core features like HTTP, database, and audio require third-party plugins that break across updates |
| No control over the renderer | You want modern lighting, post-processing, or custom shaders — impossible on a fixed 2004 engine |
| Asset pipeline constraints | You are forced into proprietary formats you can't control or extend |
| Self-hosting | You want to own your infrastructure without a platform intermediary |

## Who should not migrate

| Signal | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| Depends on base game assets | If your server relies on GTA SA/V's map, vehicles, peds, or interiors — those assets belong to Rockstar and are not part of Vital.sandbox |
| Needs the platform audience | Roblox servers lose their discovery engine, social graph, and storefront on migration |
| Wants a drop-in replacement | Vital.sandbox is a blank slate — there is no pre-loaded world, no built-in gamemode, and no compatibility layer |
