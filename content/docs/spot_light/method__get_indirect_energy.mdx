---
title: self:get_indirect_energy
description: Retrieves the indirect energy multiplier of the spot light
badge: client
---

### Syntax

```lua
local energy = self:get_indirect_energy()
```

### Parameters

| No parameters are accepted by this function |
|---------------------------------------------|

### Returns

| Type | Name | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| `float` | energy | Current indirect energy multiplier |

---

### Examples

```lua lineNumbers title="Get indirect energy of a spot light"
local entity = light.spot_light.create()

core.engine.print("info", entity:get_indirect_energy())
```
