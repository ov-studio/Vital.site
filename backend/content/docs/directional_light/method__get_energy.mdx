---
title: self:get_energy
description: Retrieves the energy (brightness multiplier) of the directional light
badge: client
---

### Syntax

```lua
local energy = self:get_energy()
```

### Parameters

| No parameters are accepted by this function |
|---------------------------------------------|

### Returns

| Type | Name | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| `float` | energy | Current energy value |

---

### Examples

```lua lineNumbers title="Get energy of a directional light"
local entity = light.directional.create()

core.engine.print("info", entity:get_energy())
```
